# Principal Odù literary review register

Owner-supplied manuscript excerpts, translations and name leads. No precise edition, page, narrator, lineage or permission was supplied. These are internal review leads, not authenticated quotations, and are not rendered in the site. Do not imply Yoruba or diaspora communities share a uniform interpretation. Verify source, spelling, translation, context and publication rights before release. Ritual prescriptions remain consultation-specific and are not published as fixed instructions. The general corpus structure is documented by UNESCO (https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/ifa-divination-system-00146). Wande Abimbola's *Ifá Divination Poetry* (1977) is a research lead, not blanket attribution (https://books.google.com/books/about/If%C3%A1_Divination_Poetry.html?id=RF9-AAAAMAAJ).

## ogbe

Name leads: Ogbè Méjì; Èjì Onílẹ̀; Baba Ejiogbe

Supplied wording: B'ẹ́jì mu, b'ẹ́jì k'àgbà... Dá fún Àkalamàgbò tí yóò tẹ Olókun-Sẹ̀níàdé n'Ifá.

Supplied translation: Drink two, give two to the elder... Divined for Àkalamàgbò who was to initiate Olókun-Sẹ̀níàdé into Ifá.

Review: Incomplete purported verse; identify edition, page and full context.

## oyeku

Name leads: Oyekun Meyi (diaspora spelling)

Supplied wording: Ọ̀pẹ̀lẹ̀ ló jẹun tán tí ó yí ikùn padà sílẹ̀, dá fún Pẹ̀rẹ̀gẹ̀dẹ́.

Supplied translation: The ọ̀pẹ̀lẹ̀ seeds that turn their bellies to the ground after being fed, divined for Pẹ̀rẹ̀gẹ̀dẹ́.

Review: Purported verse; wording varies in circulating versions. Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Logbè is not treated as an alias of Méjì.

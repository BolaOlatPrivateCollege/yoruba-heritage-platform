## Evidence, dependency and authority

Strict separate overlay fields distinguish access, source role, kind of support,
event-evidence status, edition, relationships, source target, basis record,
rights, public permission, review and correction history. Canonical guide-backed
items remain bibliographic/research leads. Draft examples distinguish curatorial
interpretation, attributed scholarship and documentary attestation. No pilot
item certifies a historical event; CI cannot grant scholarly/cultural approval.

Guide-evidence caps are explicitly bound to the original source/edition. They
cannot promote a catalogue or preview to selected-passages/full-text inspection
by changing a label or inventing a free-form page number. Draft passage pointers
must resolve to an existing on-site register. None of the pilot records is
`full-text-inspected`. The enum leaves room for a future properly documented
audit, but no current evidence basis authorises that state. New independent
inspection would require a new reviewed evidence-basis change.

Samuel Johnson’s books, including The History of the Yorubas, are not admissible
as factual authorities. The classifier is specific, not a blanket surname ban:
**Cheryl Johnson-Odim is a different scholar**. The guide notes Johnson in
Olúwọlé’s bibliography; this is a claim-level dependency caution, not proof that
all her philosophy or every other citing author is contaminated. Attributed
philosophy, oral tradition, material observations, written attestation and event
corroboration retain different scopes. No unchecked derivative historical claim
is newly admitted by this feature.

Relationship notes distinguish editions, bibliographic links and different
authors’ perspectives. More websites do not equal more independent sources.
Existing regional source-dependence groups are reused, not promoted to proof
of independent corroboration. Community scope is left unassessed where the
guides do not establish it. Authenticated, consenting, attributable Yorùbá
community perspectives remain a research priority; named practitioner books are
leads, not fabricated testimony or permission to publish lineage knowledge.

## Reader interaction and privacy

The Sources navigation retains its existing URL. The Research collection gains
a scoped link to the explorer. Four author pathways, status explanations,
source-detail overlays, original section citations and a real guided comparison
provide static reading without JavaScript. The original128-record order is
unchanged. Separate draft example disclosures use native details/summary.

Optional client filtering intersects text (author/title/institution/subject
terms), recorded access and source role. Query keys alone ignore diacritics;
display strings keep original Unicode. Controls start hidden and are revealed
only when enhanced; without scripts, all128 records and three examples remain
readable with working author/passage links. No misleading nonfunctional filter.
Filters have labels, a reset action and live result counts; empty results are
explicit. No server request, telemetry, persistence, account, mutation or intake.

The overlay defaults internal; only private-review, nonwithdrawn records render.
Withdrawn/internal canonical overlays fall back to an **unassessed original
bibliography record**, preserving protected content without exposing the overlay.
Draft pointers and the guided trace disappear when their assessment is withdrawn.
No arbitrary URL field, new uploaded content or private contacts are accepted.
Guide URLs are allowlisted. Existing noindex,nofollow remains untouched.

## Editing, correction, withdrawal and reviews

1. Edit only the separate assessment data and its grounded basis. Start new
   items internal. Confirm canonical or prior-draft source ID and scope first.
2. Record actual edition, creator, publisher, access evidence and limitations;
   do not convert a catalogue title/abstract or reachable scan into a full read.
   Obtain exact inspected locators before adding a new passage link.
3. Check the claim's upstream dependence, original-vs-mediated perspective,
   locality, privacy, sacred restrictions and permissions. Keep signed consent,
   contact data, restricted recordings and private testimony outside Git/static
   output; this task creates no intake channel.
4. Obtain qualified scholarly, Yorùbá language, community/practitioner and rights
   review where relevant. Record real reviewers; do not imply guide metadata,
   an agent assessment or CI constitutes an expert endorsement.
5. Corrections increment overlay version and append dated history. Preserve the
   prior state in Git. Underlying register corrections require their own review;
   derived passage labels then follow the registered locator.
6. Withdraw with dated reason, incremented version and withdrawal event. The
   selector removes assessment text and a withdrawn example's guided trace.
   Original canonical source metadata remains available with an unassessed
   overlay state. Do not publish sensitive withdrawal details as explanations.
7. Re-review additions before private integration. Public release is a separate
   express owner decision after appropriate human evidence/rights review.

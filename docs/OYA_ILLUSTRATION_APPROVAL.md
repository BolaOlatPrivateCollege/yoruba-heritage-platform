# Ọya — owner-approved original sepia illustration

**Owner approval:** 24 November 2026, in the project conversation.

## Illustration and provenance
The owner supplied an image of an Ọya-labelled statuette as a visual reference, and approved a **new original AI-generated artwork** showing a dynamic Ọya-inspired figure during a storm, with flowing dress and hair, an ìrùkẹ̀rẹ̀, a buffalo motif, lightning, river and distant marketplace.

This approval applies to that newly generated **sepia/monochrome** image, not to reusing the supplied reference artwork. The figure, headdress, attire, horned animal, carved lettering, dramatic weather and **cross-shaped grave marker** are artistic decisions; none are verified historical likenesses, authentic sacred regalia, or proof of a universal connection between Ọya and Christian burial symbols. The cross-shaped element needs **editorial review** and may be cropped or removed before site publication.

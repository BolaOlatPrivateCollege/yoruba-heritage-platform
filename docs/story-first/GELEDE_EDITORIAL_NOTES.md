# Gẹ̀lẹ̀dẹ́ learning tale — separate staff/editorial review notes

**Private pilot:** [Who Was the Laughing Mask Talking About?](./the-laughter-beneath-the-mask.md). This **staff-only** document is not to be displayed as the story body, appended to a child's reading page, or narrated as part of the story. Do not substitute the original Fola documentary audio for a newly written story.

## Genre and boundaries

- Original fiction, set in an imagined town with fictional Kẹ́mi, Sẹ́gun, Auntie Títí and other bystanders. The 12-second clip, online allegation, public gathering and classroom scene **were invented** for this learning tale. They are **not** a purported recorded Yorùbá local legend, authentic field interview or reconstruction of a real occurrence.
- No actual mask or specific carving is described as holding a supernatural property. An invented internet rumour is questioned without belittling spiritual and oral traditions. No private rite, sacred song, prayer, or oríkì is fabricated or reproduced.
- The story's limited factual cultural framing: Gẹ̀lẹ̀dẹ́ in Yoruba-Nago communities is a living art/performance tradition associated with masks, carving, costuming, dance, songs and satirical or playful expression, including a relationship to the honouring of mothers and women's power. A fictional character does not make this an authenticated community account.
- The distinction *one public recording does not authenticate a caption or accusation about a living person* is media-literacy fiction, not a report of actual local controversy.

## Research and cultural checks (not for learner chapter)

1. **UNESCO, [Oral heritage of Gelede](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/oral-heritage-of-gelede-00002)**. Describes the Yoruba-Nago practice in Benin, Nigeria and Togo, preparatory craftsmanship, performance and satire. Use only bounded factual framing; no reproduction of songs or recorded media.
2. **UNESCO, [Action Plan for Safeguarding and Promotion of the Oral Tradition of Gelede](https://ich.unesco.org/en/projects/action-plan-for-the-safeguarding-and-promotion-of-the-oral-tradition-of-gelede-00006)**. Notes ceremonial contexts and community roles and the importance of transmission. Does not license anonymous 'elder' quotes or a standard interpretation of all performances.
3. **UNESCO, [Oral traditions and expressions](https://ich.unesco.org/en/oral-traditions-00053)**. Emphasises oral traditions as living processes and variation in style and telling; this fits a versioned community-story experience that values actual tellers.

Do **not** cite Samuel Johnson's *History of the Yorubas* as historical authority. Do not treat imported museum descriptions or scholarly paragraphs as suitable prose for the learner story.

## Review checklist

- **Cultural sensitivity:** Have a knowledgeable, consenting Gẹ̀lẹ̀dẹ́ community practitioner or competent local researcher assess the tone and the single paragraph about mothers/women's role; no fictitious endorsement implied.
- **Child suitability:** Ask Basic 7–9 students/teachers whether the rumour, video and mystery hold attention and whether vocabulary and chapter length are accessible. Record actual feedback; do not claim classroom trials without doing them.
- **Ethical accuracy:** Ensure 'fictional town/characters/internet clip' stays clear in the story label, including when excerpted. No accusation should identify a real living trader; all names in this manuscript are invented.
- **Rights:** All prose here is original; no copied chants/music/film. Future authentic oral accounts and photos/audio require specific speaker permissions, consent and withdrawal pathways before being imported.
- **Narration:** No Fola MP3/WAV exists for this story. Existing five Fola narrations speak **different unchanged documentary texts**; never rebrand them to play as this new tale.
- **Research separation:** The student interface can offer a quiet *Explore documentary history* link to the existing scholar pages but must not show a research bibliography, archive IDs or caveat paragraphs in the story reader. Staff can retain this note privately.
- **Safety/moderation:** 'Tell us your version' is a proposed feature, **not yet active**. Do not publish a functional submission form until secure storage, private review, child safety, contributor credit and withdrawal policy are built and accepted.
- **Publication:** Keep noindex and private review until owner/editors explicitly approve both content and mechanism.

## Acceptance test of the creative direction

Ask whether a student wants to finish it, whether the humour and uncertainty feel natural, whether two readers can debate different tellings without one being silenced, whether they know how to submit a genuine account without having to pretend to be an eyewitness, and whether an adult reader finds it engaging without unnecessary scholarly interruptions. Capture the actual owner's critique before proceeding to a larger series.

# Stories, Beliefs and Research — editorial separation

**Owner direction · 23 November 2026 · Private draft; no release authorisation**

## The reader's promise

Àṣà & Ìtàn Yorùbá is a living library of stories, beliefs, sacred names, oral narratives, inherited explanations and first-hand community memories. Its **reader-facing job is to share these accounts, not to judge whether the beliefs and tales are true or false**.

Traditional practitioners, families, storytellers, elders, students and cultural historians may tell different versions. Preserve them as voices from their own contexts rather than collapsing them into one academically approved account.

### Reader areas

- **Stories & Living Memories (/tales/):** exciting folktales, legends, mysteries, historical-life stories and permissioned first-hand accounts. Strong narrative flow, respectful traditions and optional questions after the ending. No academic footnotes or evidence-rating blocks.
- **Beliefs & Figures (/figures/):** who Èṣù, Olódùmarè, Ọ̀ṣun and others are understood to be; roles, names, popular stories, practices, varied accounts and questions for local tellers. The rich Èṣù profile is the model, not a citation-heavy documentary page.
- **Places (/places/):** stories of towns, place names, families, craftsmen, markets, festivals and remembered events. Invite more than one telling from actual residents and community members.
- **Community (/community/):** a place where authorised contributors can eventually share another ending, a family memory, a belief, a first-hand account or an addition to an existing story. Different accounts can appear beside one another with permission; no vote for the one “true belief.”
- **Learning (/learning/):** language, tone, pronunciation, classroom storytelling and discussion.
- **Research (/research/):** the sole reader entry for archived source criticism, scholarly interpretations, evidence reviews, scientific comparisons, bibliographies, formal claim assessments and research-rich documentaries. Preserve original 113 articles, 128 source entries and original documentary scripts/audio, as well as stable URLs. Research methods do not impose a truth verdict onto a sacred story or lived family belief.

### The difference between a label and a verdict

Short story labels may identify **Original fiction**, **Folktale told by a named consenting person**, **Family memory**, **First-hand recollection**, **A local legend**, **A named community's sacred telling**, or **A question people ask**. This is about *who is speaking and what type of telling it is*, not a test of whether the belief is scientifically proven.

Never fabricate an elder interview or pass off an AI-generated picture as a genuine historic photo. The right to reproduce a person's words, names, portrait, recorded voice or sacred material must be respected. If the actual community/teller is not known, say so briefly rather than invent one.

### Responsibility without academic gatekeeping

Human moderators may review privacy, authorisation, copyright, risks to children, harassment, hate, or defamatory accusations about living people. They do **not** require a belief to win agreement from foreign publications or determine who is spiritually correct. Sensitive or restricted practices require proper consent and discretion. Contradictory community versions should not be overwritten simply because they differ.

### Generated images and narration

AI artwork may strengthen the storytelling with a visible **AI-generated illustration** label adjacent to the picture. It should not claim to represent an actual recorded ritual, object or photographed event. Any narration must match the exact story version. Five Fola documentary recordings belong to their original documentary transcripts until specifically approved for another use.

## Completion safeguards

- Public-facing reading areas have **no academic citation cards or “claim vs correction” panels**.
- Research remains available to users who actively choose it, without deleting or altering existing records and SEC-01 protections.
- Reader-facing pages invite first-hand voices, corrections, family stories and respectful differences while collecting no private information yet.
- Community participation waits for the independently reviewed contributor-permission, moderation and child-safety requirements in Task #32.
- No merges, public deployment, indexing, copyright imports or real submission collection from this policy change alone.

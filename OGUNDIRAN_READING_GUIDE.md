**Editorial use:** This is a research agenda, not an assertion that the website's individual claims have been validated by the book. Before citing a factual statement to Ogundiran, inspect the **specific passage and its evidence**, record the page number and evaluate whether the argument uses independent evidence rather than simply repeating an older chronicle. Site-specific claims must still be considered alongside relevant community testimony.

## Excluded source policy

At the owner's explicit instruction, Samuel Johnson's books are not admissible authorities for claims in this site. They must not be used to corroborate a story by themselves or through an unattributed secondary retelling. Because secondary historians can engage prior historiography critically, **the existence of a Johnson citation in another author's bibliography alone is not proof that the author's substantive findings rely on Johnson**. Where dependence matters, read the relevant passages and check the evidence rather than assuming either independence or dependence.

## To do before claiming full verification

- Obtain the complete legally accessible book through a library, licensed eBook or purchased copy.
- Review the relevant chapters and note page-specific evidence and any alternative interpretations.
- Check Ogundiran's original cited archaeological and oral sources where practical.
- Request regional review by Yoruba community historians and custodians.
- Update the website's exact claims and references with evidence classifications and correction dates.

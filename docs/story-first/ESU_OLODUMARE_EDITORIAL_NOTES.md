# Editor-only cultural/source notes — first Èṣù and Olódùmarè story expansion

**Private draft. This file must NOT be embedded in public tale paragraphs or treated as an authenticated community oral-history source.**

## 1. Èṣù: The Hat That Divided the Road

- **Genre:** Original literary adaptation inspired by a long-circulating Èṣù narrative motif in which two observers see different coloured sides of a traveller's hat. This exact dialogue, invented Adé/Bọ́lá characters, argument, crops and reconciliation **were written for the platform**. They have not been validated by a named Yorùbá storyteller.
- **Motif documentation:** William Bascom, *Ifa Divination* (1969), p.132 notes several Yoruba versions of Èṣù's two- or four-coloured cap; excerpt https://geomancysite.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/ifa-divination-william-bascom.pdf . This establishes a collected motif, not licence to copy text or prove any particular red/white detail.
- **Variant/community lead:** 2011 fieldwork-based discussion of Èṣù describes one account among devotees in Ọ̀yọ́ and a version reportedly collected from a devotee near Ìmẹ̀kọ́, with hat colours varying https://www.academia.edu/31337901/_LE_CONCEPT_DU_H%C3%89ROS_IMPR%C3%89VISIBLE_SYST%C3%88ME_RELIGIEUX_YOR%C3%99B%C3%81_B%C3%89NIN_NIGERIA_%C3%88%E1%B9%A2%C3%99_L_IMPROBABLE_TRICKSTER_CAHIERS_D_%C3%89TUDES_AFRICAINES_LI_4_204_pp_811_845_D%C3%A9C_2011 . This is a research lead only; original audio, consent and permission to reproduce a specific telling are outstanding.
- **Religious nuance:** Èṣù in traditional Yorùbá systems is not simply the Christian Satan, yet some Christian readers draw different theological equivalences. Do not assert that Èṣù is always benevolent, always malicious or the same in every branch of the diaspora. Avoid inventing an Ifá verse, named priest testimony or ritually authentic dialogue.
- **What the story does:** Offers a moral about perspective and accountability while clearly making the farmer dialogue *fictional*. This lesson is one choice among possible traditional interpretations, not a compulsory doctrine.
- **Community intake:** Invite permissioned local versions, name/place/dialect/variant and whether reported, witnessed, or remembered. Acknowledge different endings or meanings without treating all as equivalent factual histories. No presumed consent for online collected recordings.

## 2. Olódùmarè: The Name That Would Not Fit Into One Answer

- **Genre:** Original educational fiction with an invented teacher, friends and family members; no real grandmother/elder testimony or authenticated religious speech.
- **Referent:** Olódùmarè is broadly discussed as the supreme divine source in Yorùbá religious usage, subject to differences across speakers, denominations, regions and diaspora traditions. The name's historical etymology is **not settled**.
- **Etymology distinction:** Popular interpretations that split the word into parts such as `Oló-`, `Òdù`, `Màrè` and figures of an unfailing source are worth documenting as *interpretations* with provenance. An analysis of this kind is not automatically a historical reconstruction. Ayo Bamgbose's study https://www.africabib.org/rec.php?RID=191712140 is known bibliographically, not independently read in full. David T. Adamo (2019) https://www.scielo.org.za/scielo.php?pid=S2305-08532019000100026&script=sci_arttext discusses competing analyses, and SAGE's overview https://sk.sagepub.com/ency/edvol/embed/africanreligion/chpt/olodumare discusses religious understanding. None permits declaring one word-split indisputably true.
- **Family voice integrity:** The imagined aunt and cousin **do not** report testimony collected by the website. Their words are original fictional dialogue designed to model how learners can record who told them something. Real contributions require an identified consenting person/community or ethical agreed anonymity.
- **Claims not inserted:** No manufactured sacred verses, no categorical claim that Olódùmarè is never directly worshipped, no assertion that all Yoruba/Christian/Islamic theology is identical, no universal name-origin claim.
